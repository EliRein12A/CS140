Noah Lyles

Interest Page Hero

I wouldn't necessarily call Noah Lyles my hero but he is an athelete that is fun to watch in running. Funny enough, he also enjoys Dragon Ball Z and after winning a race, he posed doing the Kamehameha. It's a classic move used by many of the characters within Dragon Ball Z. He also has carried cards from the Yu-Gi-Oh series as he has ran which was a spectacle to see as well. Noah Lyles competing in the Olympics truly was something to root for when there were other factors that I wanted to root for too like the favorite shows I used to watch!

Olympics
An olympic gold medalist and is one of the fastest men on the earth.
Speed
Noah Lyles is incredibly fast and has at one point reached 26.1 mph.
Anime/Rapping
Has released content as a rapper and loves anime such as Dragon Ball Z and Yu-Gi-Oh.
