Dragon Ball Z

Interests Hero

I enjoy Dragon Ball Z because it's what I grew up watching with my family, It was the first show that I decided to actuallly sit down and binge with my family. Overall I enjoy action movies and shows so anything that I can watch with my family with a lot of action is pretty well received. I still continue to keep up with the show and enjoy the new content that they've been releasing. My favorite character so far has been Piccolo because he's cool and is green.

My favorite transformation within the show was Super Saiyan. It's iconic and it's what started all of the intense fighting within the show. It focuses around the main character named Goku and his journey in becoming the strongest. While focusing on getting stronger, Goku encounters many different opponents who become rivals, and enemies that he has to overcome. In facing tons of opposition in his goal of becoming the strongest, he revels at the challenges and finds new ways to overcome his obstacles.

What is a Relative Link?/Absolute Link?

A relative link is a link that allows the user to swap to a different page from their current page. They're called relative links because they are related and usually on the same website. A good example of this is this link posted below which will move up to my profile page. This page describes my interests and was created by me so this page is a relative link to that page too! On the opposite hand an absolute link is a link that leads to a completely different website or resource not similar to this. However it's good to be able to get to any specific websites that you want. Using relative you have to use ../your info and send it up whereas you can get sent to specific locations with absolute links.
