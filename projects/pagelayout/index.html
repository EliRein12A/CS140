The Series

The Percy Jackson books are awesome and they are what I grew up reading with my mom. I've always enjoyed action and adventure stories and this one is definitely one of the best. It takes the story of a young man striving to find his place in a world of Greek gods, demigods, terrifying landscapes, and treacherous perils.

The Lightning Thief story takes place as Percy figures out his identity as a demigod and gets falsely blamed for stealing Zues's lightning bolt. Along Percy's journey he encouters new allies, fights off opposing monsters, and attempts to stop a war between his father and Zeus. It sounds very complicated when trying to simplify it but it's a really good book series and I would recommend. I personally don't read anymore but if there were good movies capturing the whole series I would recommend them.

If you haven't already, please read these books!
