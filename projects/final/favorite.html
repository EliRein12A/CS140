Popularity

There are so many different board games out there and it has been debated throughout the history of time which is the most popular. According to Brandon Rollins, author of The 10 Most Popular Board Games and How They Made Gaming Better article, "The best-selling game of all-time is Chess. It's for great reasons too. Chess has variable player powers, a sophisticated area control foundation, and endless possibilities of play. While Chess may be the current #1 in terms of popularity, I want to know what your favorite game is and why?"
